Terms & Conditions

1. Purpose of data collection

The information you provide (full name, contact number, and transaction type) is collected solely to issue your queue number, notify the appropriate window, and keep a record of your visit.

2. How your data is used

Your details are shared only with the staff handling your transaction and are not used for marketing or shared with third parties.

3. Data retention

Queue records are retained for 90 days for audit purposes, then automatically deleted from our systems.

4. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your record at any time by asking the front desk staff.

5. Queue conduct

Please proceed to the assigned window when your number is called. Numbers not attended to within the posted grace period may be forfeited.
